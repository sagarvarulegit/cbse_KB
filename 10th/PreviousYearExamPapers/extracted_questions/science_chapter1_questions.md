#### Q11. [2 Marks | NCERT In-Text]
**Question:** Why is the amount of gas collected in one of the test tubes in Activity 1.7 double of the amount collected in the other? Name this gas.

* **Model Answer:** Water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$) contains 2 parts of hydrogen and 1 part of oxygen by volume. Upon electrolysis, water breaks down to yield hydrogen and oxygen in a 2:1 volume ratio. The gas collected in double quantity is Hydrogen ($\text{H}_2$).
* **Marking Scheme:** 1 mark for explaining the 2:1 ratio from chemical composition ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$). 1 mark for correctly identifying Hydrogen.

#### Q12. [2 Marks | NCERT In-Text]
**Question:** Why does the colour of copper sulphate solution change when an iron nail is dipped in it?

* **Model Answer:** Iron is more reactive than copper. It displaces copper from copper sulphate solution to form ferrous sulphate ($\text{FeSO}_4$), which is light green in colour, causing the blue color of copper sulphate to fade.  
$$\text{Fe}(s) + \text{CuSO}_4(aq) \rightarrow \text{FeSO}_4(aq) + \text{Cu}(s)$$
* **Marking Scheme:** 1 mark for explaining displacement of copper by iron. 1 mark for balanced equation or color change description (blue to green).

#### Q15. [3 Marks | Board PYQ (2025)]
**Question:** What is observed when hydrated ferrous sulphate crystals are heated in a dry boiling tube ? Give balanced chemical equation(s) of the reaction(s) that occur.

* **Model Answer:** Observation: Green crystals turn white due to loss of water of crystallisation, then turn reddish-brown solid with choking sulphur smell.

* **Marking Scheme:** 1.5 marks for complete observations. 1.5 marks for balanced equations.

#### Q16. [3 Marks | Board PYQ (2025)]
**Question:** How is a double displacement reaction different from a displacement reaction ? Explain giving example in the form of balanced chemical equations.

* **Model Answer:** Displacement involves single element displacing another ($\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \rightarrow \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$). Double displacement involves exchange of ions between two compounds ($\text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4 + \text{BaCl}_2 \rightarrow \text{BaSO}_4 + 2\text{NaCl}$).
* **Marking Scheme:** 1 mark for difference definition. 1 mark for displacement equation. 1 mark for double displacement equation.

#### Q19. [3 Marks | Board PYQ (2026)]
**Question:** Give reasons :  
(i) Potato chips manufacturers fill the packet of chips with nitrogen gas.  
(ii) Respiration is an exothermic reaction.  
(iii) Silver chloride is stored in dark coloured bottles.

* **Model Answer:**  
(i) Nitrogen creates an unreactive atmosphere, preventing oxidation of fats/oils (rancidity).  
(ii) Respiration involves oxidation of glucose releasing heat energy (ATP).  
(iii) Dark bottles prevent exposure to light, stopping photolytic decomposition of $\text{AgCl}$.
* **Marking Scheme:** 1 mark for each valid scientific reason.

**Question:**  
(a) Define the term decomposition reaction. Write one chemical equation each for decomposition reaction where energy is supplied in the form of heat, light or electricity.  
(b) Decomposition of vegetable matter into compost is considered an exothermic reaction. Why?

* **Model Answer:**  
(a) A reaction in which a single reactant breaks down into two or more simpler products.  
Heat: $\text{CaCO}_3(s) \xrightarrow{\text{Heat}} \text{CaO}(s) + \text{CO}_2(g)$  
Light: $2\text{AgCl}(s) \xrightarrow{\text{Sunlight}} 2\text{Ag}(s) + \text{Cl}_2(g)$  
Electricity: $2\text{H}_2\text{O}(l) \xrightarrow{\text{Electricity}} 2\text{H}_2(g) + \text{O}_2(g)$  
(b) Composting involves microbes breaking down complex organic molecules, releasing heat energy into the surroundings.
* **Marking Scheme:** 1M definition, 3M for 3 balanced equations with state symbols and conditions, 1M for composting reason mentioning energy release.
